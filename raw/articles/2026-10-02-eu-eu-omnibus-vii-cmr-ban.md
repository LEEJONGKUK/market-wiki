## Key excerpts
> "from 1 September 2025 non-compliant cosmetics cannot be sold on the EU market anymore and must be recalled before."

## Notes
- Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/877, published 2025-05-13, amends Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009.
- Adds 22 CMR substances to Annex II (prohibited); applies from 2025-09-01.
- TPO (CAS 75980-60-8, gel nail photoinitiator) moved from restricted to fully prohibited.
- No sell-through period.
