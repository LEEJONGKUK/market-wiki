## Key excerpts
> "Manufacturers of packed goods and packaging must have the relevant Technical Documentation and Declaration of Conformity"

## Notes
- Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2025/40; applies from 2026-08-12 (some provisions later).
- Covers cosmetics packaging; topics: substances in packaging (Art. 5), recyclability/recycled content (Art. 6-7), minimisation (Art. 10), reuse (Art. 11), labelling (Art. 12).
